What this prototype is testing
  • A: the coil check sits on the monthly sheet itself, next to Planned, before Finalise.
  • B: a separate coil desk by plant × thickness, with coil on order typed in and an HR buy list.
  • C: a mid-month checkpoint, in three steps: what changed since the 1st → the re-plan → the coil check again.
  • Re-plan rule used here: add = (orders pending now − stock now − plan still to make), rounded up to 10, never below 0.
  • Coil rule as built today: fitting slit coil (same plant, same band, strip ± 5 mm) first, then HR coil of the band, in week order. Coil on order is not recorded anywhere yet.
