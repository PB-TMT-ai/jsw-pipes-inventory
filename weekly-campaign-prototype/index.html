STAGE 1 · ONCE, KEPT UP TO DATE

Planning master

Admin only, edited on screen
  • Decided One row per Size × Thickness × Region, with Section, Buffer or MTO, Buffer T, and ticks for which plants can make it. 471 rows: South 236, West 235. #249
  • Off-band thicknesses roll into the nearest band (2.8 / 2.9 → 3.0; 1.8 / 2.2 → 2.0). The SKU master is not touched. #258
  • A row no plant can make stays on the master and shows red as "No plant can make". It is never auto-ticked. #250
  • 154 of 236 South pairs match a catalogue SKU. Stock in no row is listed as "Outside the plan". #251
STAGE 2 · START OF MONTH

Monthly campaign (region)

Planner types the Monthly Indent
  • Decided The Monthly Indent is typed by hand, never taken from the ERP order book.
  • Open = finished stock on the 1st, not netted against orders. Oct and Aug–Oct = invoiced T by shipping plant, for judgement only. #260
  • Suggested: Buffer row = max(Indent, Buffer) − Open; MTO row = Indent − Open. Never below 0, rounded up to 10. The planner may type over it. #252
  • Screen A2: one light sheet per region, 9 columns (Size · Thk · Buffer · Open · Oct · Aug–Oct · Indent · Planned · Plant). #254
STAGE 3 · BEFORE THE MONTH RUNS

Plant-wise plan + Finalise

Planner assigns; admin finalises
  • Decided Each row goes to one ticked plant, or Split… across ticked plants (red until the split adds up). One-option rows are auto-assigned. Two-option rows start on last month's plant. #253
  • Finalise per region per month. It is blocked while a row needs a plant. The figures at that moment are kept as the Baseline. #255
  • The plan stays editable after Finalise. Every change is logged with a required reason. There is no Un-finalise.
  • A plant's campaign = the rows assigned to it + its share of split rows.
STAGE 4 · THROUGH THE MONTH

Weekly campaign + coil

Planner adjusts; plants read
  • Decided The app proposes the weeks: size, then thin → thick, whole rows. The planner moves a row or splits it across weeks (red until it adds up). #273
  • Region overview, then one plant's rows in W1–W4. What a closed week didn't make stays red until moved. Moves are logged automatically, with no reason asked.
  • Decided Coil per row: slit coil that fits (strip width from the tube's size, ± 5 mm), then HR coil of the band. On hand only, handed out in week order, 1 : 1. A thickness line shows "short X by ". #276

Rules that never bend

A plant only serves its own region. Allocation never crosses plants. Plant capacity is not checked anywhere (#272, Out of scope), so the weekly split cannot use a capacity figure.
